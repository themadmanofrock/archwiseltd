# Archwise Ltd. — Business Copy Brief

Use the **LLM Style and Copy Guide, Version 3: Business Edition** for this task.

Write for **intelligent prospective clients, collaborators, and creative-business decision-makers who prefer direct substance to polished corporate theater**. Produce **business-facing website, proposal, profile, or announcement copy, adapted to the requested platform** under **Archwise Ltd.**

The purpose is to help the reader **understand the specific offer supported by the source notes, why it matters in practical terms, and what—if anything—they should do next.**

Use my notes as the sole source of facts. Preserve their substance, constraints, and necessary detail. Where the notes do not establish a fact, label it **[MISSING: …]** rather than filling the gap with a plausible claim.

## Voice: Make It Yours

Write in an original voice that is:
- blunt, intelligent, and alive;
- dark-edged without becoming melodramatic;
- candid about uncertainty and allergic to corporate varnish;
- specific enough to withstand scrutiny;
- human in rhythm—occasional sharp turns, dry humor, and a refusal to sound focus-grouped.

This is not celebrity imitation. Do not reproduce or imitate any living person’s voice, wording, catchphrases, or persona.

Replace generic praise, startup posturing, engagement bait, and stock LLM patterns with concrete wording. Do not invent experience, testing, quotations, credentials, clients, features, results, or outcomes. Verify time-sensitive facts when they materially matter.

Include a call to action only when it serves the stated purpose. Keep source notes and editorial flags separate from finished copy.
